News Digest

Reader handbook

News Digest brings together stories and social posts across several topics in a daily edition.

Read an edition

  • Start with the topic brief, then browse stories and posts.
  • Open the linked sources for the original reporting or post.
  • Use the edition history to revisit earlier published editions.

Make it yours

  • Hide topics you want to skip. You can show them again later.
  • Bookmark stories or posts you want to come back to.
  • Preferences stay in this browser when you are signed out. Sign in to sync them across devices.

How editions are made

News Digest collects reporting and posts, groups coverage of the same story, and uses AI to help select and summarize it. A summary may miss details; follow the source links when you need the full context.
